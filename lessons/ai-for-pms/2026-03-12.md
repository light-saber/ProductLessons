# LLM Models in 2026: The PM's Buyer's Guide

**Topic:** LLM Models (GPT, Claude, Gemini, Llama) — Capabilities, Costs, Tradeoffs  
**Date:** March 12, 2026

---

## 1. Technical Explanation

The four major LLM families have converged on similar capabilities but diverge sharply on **pricing, ecosystem lock-in, and deployment flexibility**.

| Model | Input $/1M tokens | Output $/1M tokens | Context Window |
|---|---|---|---|
| GPT-5.4 | $2.50 | $15.00 | 1M tokens |
| Claude Opus 4.6 | $5.00 | $25.00 | 1M tokens |
| Gemini 3 Pro | $2.00 | $12.00 | 1M tokens |
| Gemini 3 Flash | $0.50 | $3.00 | 1M tokens |
| Llama 4 (hosted) | ~$0.05–$0.90 | ~$0.08–$0.90 | 10M tokens |

Key differentiators: **Claude** leads in coding and agentic tasks. **GPT-5.4** excels at reasoning with its "Thinking" layer. **Gemini** dominates multimodal and Google ecosystem integration. **Llama** is open-weight — free to download, you pay only for compute, and you own the deployment.

Cost multipliers to watch: long-context surcharges (2x beyond 200K tokens on most models), output tokens costing 3–5x input tokens, and cached-input discounts (up to 90% on repeat prompts). A PM who ignores these asymmetries will blow the budget on day one.

---

## 2. Simple Explanation

Think of choosing an LLM like picking a phone carrier. **GPT** is like the premium carrier — great coverage, polished experience, mid-range pricing. **Claude** is the one engineers love — best for technical work, slightly pricier. **Gemini** is deeply bundled with Google apps, like choosing the carrier that gives you free YouTube Premium. **Llama** is buying an unlocked phone — no monthly bill, but you manage everything yourself.

The real cost isn't just the sticker price. It's like mobile data: sending a question (input) is cheap, but the AI's reply (output) costs 3–5x more. Long conversations cost extra too. Smart PMs design products that keep prompts short and cache repeated context.

---

## 3. Real Example

At **Walmart**, imagine building an AI assistant for store associates to check inventory and answer policy questions. A PM must choose:

- **Gemini 3 Flash** ($0.50/$3.00 per M tokens) for high-volume, simple queries like "Is item X in stock?" — cheap and fast.
- **Claude Opus 4.6** for complex tasks like analyzing supplier contracts or debugging internal tools — expensive but accurate.
- **Llama 4 self-hosted** for sensitive employee data queries where data can't leave Walmart's infrastructure.

The winning move? **Route by complexity.** 80% of queries hit the cheap model, 15% the mid-tier, 5% the premium. This "model routing" pattern can cut costs by 60–70% versus sending everything to one model.

---

## 4. Key Takeaways

- **Output tokens cost 3–5x more than input** — design UIs that constrain response length
- **Model routing** (cheap model for simple tasks, premium for hard ones) is the single biggest cost lever
- **Open-weight models (Llama)** aren't free — compute costs add up — but they give you data sovereignty
- **Long-context surcharges double your costs** beyond 200K tokens; architect around this limit
- **No single model wins everything** — the PM's job is matching the right model to each use case
