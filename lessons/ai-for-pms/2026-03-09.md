# Inference Infrastructure: Where Your AI Budget Actually Goes

**Topic:** Inference Infrastructure (GPUs, TPUs, Edge Deployment)  
**Date:** March 9, 2026  
**Cycle:** 2 | Lesson 11

---

## 1. Technical Explanation

Inference — running a trained model to generate outputs — now accounts for **~two-thirds of all AI compute** and 80-90% of a model's lifetime cost. Training gets the headlines; inference gets the bill.

The hardware landscape is fragmenting. NVIDIA's H100/H200/B200 GPUs dominate, but Google's TPUs, Amazon's Trainium/Inferentia chips, and AMD's MI355 are carving out niches. These ASICs trade generality for efficiency — Google's TPU v5e delivers inference at roughly **half the cost-per-token** of equivalent GPU setups for specific workloads.

Edge deployment (on-device inference) is the next frontier. Techniques like **4-bit quantization**, pruning, and distillation shrink models to run on phones and IoT devices. Apple's on-device models and Qualcomm's NPUs already handle ~3B parameter models locally.

The cost trajectory is striking: GPT-4-equivalent inference dropped from **$20/million tokens (late 2022) to ~$0.40 (early 2026)** — a 50x reduction. But total inference spend keeps rising (Jevons Paradox: cheaper = more usage).

## 2. Simple Explanation

Think of AI infrastructure like a restaurant kitchen. **Training** is developing the recipe — expensive, but you do it once. **Inference** is cooking every single order — that's where the ongoing cost lives.

GPUs are your industrial ovens — powerful, expensive, versatile. **Custom chips (ASICs)** are like a dedicated pizza oven — cheaper per pizza, but that's all it does. **Edge deployment** is like giving every customer a microwave — limited menu, but instant, private, and no delivery cost.

As a PM, you don't pick the chips, but you need to know: your choice of model size and deployment target (cloud vs. edge vs. hybrid) determines whether your feature costs ₹0.03 or ₹3 per user interaction.

## 3. Real Example

Consider Walmart's in-store associate tools. Running a full GPT-4-class model in the cloud for every barcode scan query would cost millions annually across 10,000+ stores. Instead, the PM decision is: deploy a **quantized 7B model on edge devices** in stores for common queries (inventory checks, product info), and route only complex questions to cloud inference.

This hybrid architecture cuts latency from ~2 seconds to ~200ms for 80% of queries, reduces cloud costs by 60%, and works even when store internet is spotty. The PM didn't need to pick the GPU — but they needed to know that a quantized small model could handle the simple cases, making the business case viable.

## 4. Key Takeaways

- **Inference is 80-90% of lifetime AI compute cost** — optimize here, not just training
- **Hardware is diversifying** — ASICs and custom chips break NVIDIA's monopoly, creating cost leverage for PMs
- **Edge vs. cloud is a product decision**, not just an infra decision — it affects latency, privacy, offline capability, and unit economics
- **Cost per token is crashing** (~50x reduction in 3 years), but total spend rises because usage explodes
- **Hybrid architectures win** — small models on edge for common cases, big models in cloud for hard cases
